- `prose` groups things by spacing, so a page can be skimmed: a new section (`h2`) sits further from the last, a heading sits closer to what it heads, and a list, code block or callout sits closer to the paragraph that introduces it.
- `prose` makes bold full weight, and styles an `aside` as a callout, with an accent bar and a soft accent background.
- New `sheet`: a quieter window for each section of a long page, an outline and a surface without a `Panel`'s title bar or shadow. Inside `prose`, sheets are spaced as sections, with the same spacing inside each, and a `type-eyebrow` above a sheet's heading sits close to it.
- New `bg-clearing`: a clear patch of canvas behind text that fades into the grid on every side, so the grid's lines don't run through a column of text.
